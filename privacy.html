1. Who handles your payment

Your payment is processed by WarriorPlus, not by me. WarriorPlus acts as the merchant of record: they take your money, issue the receipt, and handle the payment details. Your card number is never sent to me and I never see it.

Because WarriorPlus processes the transaction, they are also the party that provides transaction-related data — such as your name, billing address, and order details — to me so I can deliver your purchase and provide support. That is the only reason transaction data reaches me at all.

2. What is collected

Two categories of information exist in connection with this product.

Transaction data (held by WarriorPlus, passed to me for fulfilment)

  • Your name and the email address used at checkout
  • Your order details, including what you bought and when
  • Billing address, where required for tax handling by the payment processor
  • Payment card details — held by the payment processor, never by me

Data I create myself

  • Which product you purchased, and any support correspondence we exchange
  • No behavioural or tracking data is collected by me for advertising purposes

4. AI tools and the prompts you paste into them

Read this one carefully. These prompts are designed to be pasted into third-party artificial intelligence tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and similar services. Once you paste a prompt into one of those services, your copy of that service's privacy policy governs what happens next — not this page and not me.

Those providers may process your text to run the model, may retain it, and may in some cases use it to improve their services. You decide which tool to use and what to paste. I have no access to, and no visibility into, anything you submit to them.

The prompts themselves are my content. They contain no personal data about you, no credentials, and no tracking code. They are not sent anywhere by themselves — they only act when you paste them.

Where advertising or analytics cookies are used, they are subject to your browser's consent controls and to the policies of the provider operating them. I do not run advertising pixels on this page, and I do not build a profile of you across other websites.

I do not sell your personal information. I do not share it for anyone else's marketing.
