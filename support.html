1. How to contact me

Payment problems go through WarriorPlus, not to me — they process the money. But email me anyway if you are stuck and I will point you to the right place.

2. Check these first

Most questions are already answered here, and a quick check usually solves it:

  • Which AI tool is best? Any model with a large context window. The five-step build has to remember every earlier step, so a long chat window matters far more than raw speed. A desktop agent with a persistent workspace is smoothest.
  • How do I keep Book 2 consistent with Book 1? Copy the "What's Now True" block at the end of each book into the start of the next one. That block is the continuity mechanism.
  • Do I have to fill in any fields? No. Every row is a complete prompt. Copy the cell, paste it, run it.
  • It stopped mid-book. That is by design. Say Continue and it picks up at the next chapter boundary.
  • My output contradicts an earlier book. You probably skipped the "What's Now True" carry-forward block. That is the fix.

3. What to include in your email

These four things mean I can usually answer in one go:

  1. Your order reference from the receipt
  2. Which AI tool and version you are using
  3. Which step you are on, and what you expected
  4. The relevant text — a short excerpt is fine, no need to paste everything

If something is broken, corrupt, or missing, say so plainly in the first line. That gets it moved to the front.

4. Response time

I answer every message personally. Realistically that means within 1 to 3 business days, usually the same day. If something is on fire, say so in the subject line.

I do not offer 24/7 support because I am one person and I would rather tell you the truth about that than pretend otherwise.
