Why This, and Why Now

336 Agentic Prompts for Post-Apocalyptic Saga Builders covers eight apocalypse types. This pack adds six it does not contain, and not by stretching the same ground. Drought & Dust, Solar Flare & EMP, Bioweapon & Genetic, Undead Plague, Volcanic Winter and Deep Space Colony Collapse are all new — different settings, different physics, and different problems for a protagonist to be trying to solve.

The reason the count matters is continuity. A saga rarely dies from a weak premise. It dies at Book 2, when the rules you established stop holding and the model you are working with has no memory of the world you already built. That is a bookkeeping failure, not a writing one.

All 727 of these close every finished book with a What's Now True summary of the world as it now stands. Paste it forward, and Book 4 inherits the world you made in Book 1. That is the whole difference between a prompt you run once and a saga you can actually finish.
